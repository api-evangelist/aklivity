AI production systems fail due to infrastructure challenges rather than model limitations. Apache Kafka naturally addresses asynchronous operations, backpressure, and replayability that AI pipelines require, while Zilla Platform adds governance capabilities like JWT validation, schema enforcement, and multi-tenant access control at the API boundary.
